## Dynamic pluvial tank

```text
dV/dt  = TANK_K · max(0, R(t − lag) − D(bay))^TANK_GAMMA
          − TANK_KOUT · V
stage  = stage_storage_curve(fill from tide-set base, +V)
water  = 3.52 + stage/12          (NAVD88)

TANK_K       = 1.296e6 cell-in/h at 1 in/hr net
TANK_GAMMA   = 0.78
TANK_KOUT    = 3.50 /h            (~17-minute reservoir e-folding)
TANK_LAG_MIN = 15 min
D(bay)       = head-dependent drain (0.25 in/hr below bay 3.0,
               tapering to 0 at bay 3.52)
```

Unchanged from v0.10.1 (measured-parameter refit; see
`archive/v0.10.1.md` for the fit provenance and the reproducibility
caveat about the original optimizer trace). The input is raw hourly
NWS QPF for forecasts and observed MRMS PrecipRate for nowcasts. The
tank supplies lag, integration, and recession. QPF can smear or miss
convection, so validated tank physics is not the same as validated
rain input skill.

## Tide-keyed pathway

For each high tide:

```text
SH peak MLLW = astronomical tide + NWS projection or observed surge
water NAVD88 = SH peak MLLW + 0.00 − 2.82
depth at landmark = max(0, water − landmark NAVD88) × 12 inches
```

If live surge is unavailable, production labels the calculation
`astronomical-only-degraded` rather than calling a zero departure surge
persistence. Offshore peak winds retain the separately reported −0.13 ft
expected-actual adjustment. The retired cold-lockout remains advisory only.

## Static scenarios

The map slider, rain calculator, and advisory scenarios use the tank's steady
state with the same constants:

```text
V_steady = (TANK_K / TANK_KOUT) · net_rate^TANK_GAMMA
```

The saturating tanh estimate remains a labeled conservative comparison. The
v0.9 power-law remains available only as a legacy comparison; neither drives
the primary hydrograph.

## Calibration evidence and limitations

- Measured peak anchors: six — December 19 and October 30, 2025; July 6,
  July 9, July 18, and August 3, 2026. (Post-cutover measured events —
  August 7 and September 1, 2026 — are tracked as ‡ recipe hindcasts in
  the all-anchors figure, outside this frozen set.)
- Full fit hydrographs: two — July 6 and July 9, 2026.
- Independent peak checks inherited from v0.10: October 30 and December 19,
  2025.
- Independent measured recession constraint: one — Event #5, July 18, 2026.
- Out-of-sample hindcast: August 3, 2026 (13.4 inches modeled versus the
  13.7–13.9-inch measured crest band; model constants unchanged).
- `driveway_central` is cross-fit [INFERRED], not surveyed — see "What
  changed" above; it participates in evidence bounds, not in the tank
  fit.
- Frozen reproduction command:
  [`history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_1.py`](../history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_1.py).
  It reads [`model/data/v0.10.1-reproduction.json`](data/v0.10.1-reproduction.json),
  verifies the production vector, 24-point RMS, six event hindcasts, and
  model-version cutover, and writes no production files.

Known structural gaps remain deliberately untuned: antecedent catchment
wetting, bidirectional drains and hysteresis, a tilted recession pool,
recirculation between grates, a long hillside delivery tail, and the sidewalk
swale micro-basin. Future events should test these structures without silently
changing this version's constants. The `_pluvial_fill` sub-bin quantization
(BACKLOG) also remains: fixing it changes outputs and is reserved for its own
assessed version bump with goldens regenerated in lockstep.
