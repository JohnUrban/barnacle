Hourly-run acquisition: five quick sources (seven HTTP requests: the grid
and the cross-check each make two) under one 60-s monotonic deadline, 15 s
cap per request, with the timeout recomputed from the deadline before EVERY
request, nested ones included; a spent budget raises before the next request.
The quick fetches run in a worker thread that the run waits on for at most a
**75-s wall clock** (budget + one cap); anything unfinished is reported
"unavailable: wall-clock budget" and abandoned. So the alert path waits at
most 75 s on the optional outlook acquisition (the workflow's 5-minute
timeout is the outer bound); it never waits on NOMADS, which the warm job
(`data/outlook_guidance.json`, every 3 h) owns.

### Precedence

- **Outlook per high tide (outlook cards, table, shadow ledger):** nws_product → nwps (≤ 72 h, shadow)
  → petss midpoint (≤ 102 h, band shown) → persistence decayed e^(−lead/48 h)
  (assumption, shadow-scored) → astronomy labeled "no surge guidance".
- **Outlook continuous hourly line (outlook chart and extended maps):** CFW
  rows correct the NWPS hourly shape. At each product tide the correction is
  product total minus the larger bracketing NWPS hourly value; corrections
  interpolate between product times. Outside the first/last product time the
  correction is held for 1 h, then fades to zero over a further 6 h. This is
  an approximate hourly anchoring rule, not exact reconstruction of every
  sub-hourly tide peak. Beyond NWPS: astronomy + interpolated outlook-ladder surge.
- **Rain per hour:** NWS grid inside its reach → NBM 6-h uniform rate → WPC
  24-h uniform rate → unknown (flagged, never zero). Buckets are [start, end).
  Day totals are prorated by overlap with the station-local day.
- **Production per high tide:** product row within 2 h, else surge persistence,
  else astronomical-only-degraded (unchanged).
- **Production continuous curve (September 23 recovery):** latest despiked,
  age-validated observed surge held constant across the 30-h curve and its rain
  tank. Never the worst product tide's surge. Missing/stale observed surge leaves
  the continuous series unavailable rather than supplying a numeric bay/tank
  forecast; explicit zero remains valid for the nowcast astronomy-only caller.
  INTERIM: John decided (2026-09-23, BACKLOG `missing-surge-ladder`,
  `missing-surge-ladder-decay`) to replace this with an age-labeled ladder
  whose stale surge decays toward the recent average; that is v0.10.6 work.
  `water_series_input` records source, value, observation timestamp, age and
  status. `current_surge_ft` retains its legacy worst-tide meaning.
- **Scoped health:** `input_health` retains every source; `degraded_inputs`
  lists only production failures and `outlook_degraded_inputs` only outlook
  failures. Outlook pages/maps display their own failures; core email/widget
  do not warn about unused outlook inputs. Maps label the source transition.

### Horizon and scope
- Outlook: a ROLLING 168 h from the run instant. The hourly series runs from
  −6 h to +168 h; the tide table lists every high tide ≤ 168 h; the slider
  reaches the last series point. Day cards cover every station-local calendar
  date the horizon touches (eight in general); the first and last are marked
  partial with their hours in scope, and a partial last card may hold no high
  tide. `horizon_hours` (168) is the requested horizon; the final hourly
  sample can be less than one hour short of that boundary.
- Unknown rain: an hour with no rain forecast is flagged `rain_unknown` in the
  series, `u` in the map payload, gray on the chart, and labeled "tide-only,
  not a flood forecast" at that instant on both maps. Day-card rain coverage
  counts only VALID intervals: explicit null intervals are unknown hours
  (`qpf_null_h`); day-total aggregation assumes absent intervals inside the
  NWS grid's reach are dry. Hourly rain lookup still tries NBM/WPC for missing
  values. `qpf_partial` marks coverage short of that local day's actual length
  by more than 0.5 h (including 23/25-hour DST days).
- Alerts (ntfy/email): tides ≤ 48 h ahead only (`ALERT_WINDOW_HOURS`); SMS is
  nowcast-only. Display horizons (72-h rollup, 7-day outlook) never widen
  alerting.

### Rain scenarios
- Tank line: the production tank integrated on the tide+surge curve.
- Burst scenario: analog 1.7 × (max 6-h / 0.55) capped 3.0 in/hr when
  burst-capable hours or convective wording exist; the max 6-h amount is
  taken over timestamped 6-h windows and flagged when the window is
  incomplete. Three NAMED scenarios: (i) rain-alone at the fixed 2.5 ft
  NAVD88 low bay (cards); (ii) time-local compound, the burst at each
  burst-capable hour's own tide level (chart band, both maps' per-point
  potential, and the "worst flood chance" selection); (iii) hypothetical
  burst on the day's high tide (cards only; it has no clock, is not drawn
  on the chart and is never the timed worst selection).
- NBM 90th-percentile scenario: the same analog on the p90 6-h amount, shown
  as the rain band's labeled high end with its exceedance chance; it does not
  drive the headline (mirrors the P-ETSS high end).
- Summed 6-h percentiles are not daily percentiles and are named as sums.

### Day-scoped worst pathway (headline rule, AGENTS rule 6)
`day_worst` = max over {tide regime of the day's tides; regime of the day's
highest continuous water; burst-potential regime on burst-capable hours;
categorical rain watch at rank 1}, computed from the run instant FORWARD
(tides and series points at or after `generated_utc`; past water stays in
the labeled lookback). Landing cards, ribbon, today's headline, and the
email subject, text and HTML "WORST 72 H" panel all read the same result
through one function. Widget keeps its owner-set tidal-days / rain-days
lines. The former residual (the 30-h series carrying the worst product
tide's surge on every hour) was removed by the 2026-09-23 recovery
(`eb8e79475`): the curve uses observed-surge persistence.

### Shadow scoring and promotion policy
`data/outlook_log.csv` (append-only) logs every source per tide per run.
Scoring unit: one issued forecast of one tide. Within each lead bucket,
absolute errors are averaged within a tide first, then across tides with
equal weight (MAE); signed errors the same way, separately (bias).
Candidate and baseline are paired only on the same ledger row (same
issuance and target); `n` = distinct tides, `n_obs` = tide-bucket pairs,
`n_forecasts` = paired rows. READY requires 28 distinct tides and a lower
MAE; promotion of any shadow source into production remains human-gated
(rule 12).

### Confidence labels
Retired from every human surface and from `forecast.json`; ledgers keep the
column and write it empty; the measured error by lead replaces them, named
as Sandy Hook tide-peak gauge skill.

## Dynamic pluvial tank

```text
dV/dt  = TANK_K · max(0, R(t − lag) − D(bay))^TANK_GAMMA
          − TANK_KOUT · V
stage  = corrected_stage_storage_inverse(base_stage, +V)
water  = 3.52 + stage/12          (NAVD88)

TANK_K       = 1.296e6 cell-in/h at 1 in/hr net
TANK_GAMMA   = 0.78
TANK_KOUT    = 3.50 /h            (~17-minute reservoir e-folding)
TANK_LAG_MIN = 15 min
D(bay)       = head-dependent drain (0.25 in/hr below bay 3.0,
               tapering to 0 at bay 3.52)
```

Unchanged from v0.10.3 (which corrected only the sub-bin stage-storage
inverse; constants from the v0.10.1 refit). Forecasts use raw hourly NWS
QPF; nowcasts use observed MRMS PrecipRate over the catchment. Validated
tank arithmetic is not validated rain-input skill. The nowcast still
holds one bay head through its 45-minute projection; the moving-head
candidate remains HELD (see `archive/v0.10.3.md`).

## Tide-keyed pathway

For each high tide:

```text
SH peak MLLW = matching NWS product TOTAL, when available
            = astronomical tide + observed surge, otherwise
            = astronomical tide, if neither input is available (degraded)
water NAVD88 = SH peak MLLW + 0.00 − 2.82
depth at landmark = max(0, water − landmark NAVD88) × 12 inches
```

If live surge is unavailable, production labels the calculation
`astronomical-only-degraded`. Offshore peak winds retain the separately
reported −0.13-ft expected-actual adjustment. The retired cold lockout
remains advisory only.

## Static scenarios

The map slider, rain calculator, and advisory scenarios use the same tank
constants at steady state and the corrected stage-storage inverse:

```text
V_steady = (TANK_K / TANK_KOUT) · net_rate^TANK_GAMMA
```

The saturating tanh estimate remains a labeled conservative comparison.
The v0.9 power law remains only as a legacy comparison; neither drives
the primary hydrograph.
