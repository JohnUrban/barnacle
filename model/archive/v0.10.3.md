# Bay Ave Barnacle — Flood Prediction Model v0.10.3

**Promotion date:** 2026-09-18. Stage-storage inversion correctness release —
**no fitted constant, landmark, input, lag, pathway, or alert-policy change**.
The superseded specification is preserved at
[`archive/v0.10.2.md`](v0.10.2.md).

## What changed from v0.10.2

`_pluvial_fill()` now starts the first stage-storage interval at the exact
tide-set base stage when that base falls inside a 0.1-inch curve bin. v0.10.2
skipped bins below the base but charged the first surviving bin from its lower
edge; with tiny positive rain storage it could therefore return a stage up to
0.090 inch below the starting water level.

The corrected inverse is equivalent to:

```text
final_stage = inverse_curve(volume_at(base_stage) + rain_storage)
```

It matches that independent reference to 1.07e-14 inch across 31,213 sampled
base/storage combinations. Frozen effects are deliberately small:

- July 6, July 9, July 18, and August 3 peaks: bit-identical.
- October 30 peak: 20.8802 → 20.9431 inches (+0.0629 inch).
- December 19 peak: 14.2400 → 14.2542 inches (+0.0142 inch).
- December 19 observation-time stage: 11.0077 → 11.0279 inches (+0.0202
  inch), still inside the 10.1–12.2-inch observed band.
- All six frozen peak clocks: unchanged.

No parameter was refit. The production reproduction command is
[`history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_3.py`](../../history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_3.py),
backed by
[`model/data/v0.10.3-reproduction.json`](../data/v0.10.3-reproduction.json).

## Dynamic pluvial tank

```text
dV/dt  = TANK_K · max(0, R(t − lag) − D(bay))^TANK_GAMMA
          − TANK_KOUT · V
stage  = corrected_stage_storage_inverse(base_stage, +V)
water  = 3.52 + stage/12          (NAVD88)

TANK_K       = 1.296e6 cell-in/h at 1 in/hr net
TANK_GAMMA   = 0.78
TANK_KOUT    = 3.50 /h            (~17-minute reservoir e-folding)
TANK_LAG_MIN = 15 min
D(bay)       = head-dependent drain (0.25 in/hr below bay 3.0,
               tapering to 0 at bay 3.52)
```

The constants and forcing are unchanged from v0.10.1. Forecasts use raw
hourly NWS QPF; nowcasts use observed MRMS PrecipRate over the catchment.
Validated tank arithmetic is not validated rain-input skill: QPF can smear or
miss convection.

The nowcast still holds one bay head through its 45-minute projection.
Offline replay found that moving astronomy plus constant issue-time surge is
not universally better when surge itself changes rapidly, so that candidate
is explicitly **not** part of v0.10.3.

## Tide-keyed pathway

For each high tide:

```text
SH peak MLLW = astronomical tide + NWS projection or observed surge
water NAVD88 = SH peak MLLW + 0.00 − 2.82
depth at landmark = max(0, water − landmark NAVD88) × 12 inches
```

If live surge is unavailable, production labels the calculation
`astronomical-only-degraded`. Offshore peak winds retain the separately
reported −0.13-ft expected-actual adjustment. The retired cold lockout
remains advisory only.

## Static scenarios

The map slider, rain calculator, and advisory scenarios use the same tank
constants at steady state and the corrected stage-storage inverse:

```text
V_steady = (TANK_K / TANK_KOUT) · net_rate^TANK_GAMMA
```

The saturating tanh estimate remains a labeled conservative comparison. The
v0.9 power law remains only as a legacy comparison; neither drives the
primary hydrograph.

## Calibration evidence and limitations

- Measured peak anchors: six — December 19 and October 30, 2025; July 6,
  July 9, July 18, and August 3, 2026.
- Full fit hydrographs: two — July 6 and July 9, 2026.
- Independent peak checks: October 30 and December 19, 2025.
- Independent measured recession constraint: one — July 18, 2026.
- Out-of-sample hindcast: August 3, 2026.
- Post-cutover measured Events 7–9 remain recipe hindcasts, not additions to
  the frozen calibration set.
- `driveway_central` is cross-fit [INFERRED], not surveyed; it participates
  in evidence bounds, not the tank fit.

Known structural gaps remain deliberately untuned: event-dependent lag,
antecedent wetting, bidirectional drainage and hysteresis, a tilted recession
pool, recirculation between grates, long hillside delivery, and the sidewalk
swale micro-basin. The moving-head constant-surge prototype is held pending a
bounded surge-tendency and expiry contract. Future changes must be evaluated
one mechanism at a time and versioned explicitly.
