## What changed from v0.10.3

`driveway_central` is **removed from the landmark ladder** (19 → 18).
It had been registered in v0.10.2 at 4.67 ft NAVD88 as a "threshold
observable" and carried through v0.10.3. The owner's 2026-09-20 review
found the registration did not meet the standard the other landmarks
meet, and the record agrees:

- A landmark is a fixed physical point anyone can report as *under /
  level with / over*. "Water entering the driveway" is interpretive —
  a two-car ramp climbing from the curb cut past the sidewalk to the
  garage, with no defined observation point.
- 4.67 ft is not the elevation of any locatable point. It was a
  corner-stage association (+13.8–13.9″ at the SW grate) that
  coincided with three loose driveway-wet reports of different kinds
  (a mud absence after #6, one photo from #8, one witness sentence on
  8/27); event #9 showed water on the lower ramp at +13.7″, outside
  that bracket. The surveyed road beside the driveway
  (`driveway_road_central`, `assets/map_points.csv`) is 4.11 ft — a
  seven-inch disagreement that no one can resolve by walking outside.
- Displayed as a peer of the landmarks, it sat 0.1″ between the lawn
  step (4.66) and the porch base (4.68) and could displace "Lawn
  step" as the widget's highest-exceeded rung.

The driveway remains useful **as a proxy**, and is kept as one: the
residue doctrine in `PLAYBOOK.md` now states the PROXIES vs LANDMARKS
rule, with the driveway as its type example (corner stage ≈
+13.7–13.9″ coincided with driveway-wet reports in #6, #8, #9 and
8/27 — a weak lower bound, [INFERRED], never a rung). Ledger rows keep
the `driveway_central` key (append-only); `data/labeled_observations_README.md`
documents it among the non-model keys. To make it a landmark someday:
pick a physical point on the ramp, mark it, and tape it — the method
that built the rest of the ladder.

## Dynamic pluvial tank

```text
dV/dt  = TANK_K · max(0, R(t − lag) − D(bay))^TANK_GAMMA
          − TANK_KOUT · V
stage  = corrected_stage_storage_inverse(base_stage, +V)
water  = 3.52 + stage/12          (NAVD88)

TANK_K       = 1.296e6 cell-in/h at 1 in/hr net
TANK_GAMMA   = 0.78
TANK_KOUT    = 3.50 /h            (~17-minute reservoir e-folding)
TANK_LAG_MIN = 15 min
D(bay)       = head-dependent drain (0.25 in/hr below bay 3.0,
               tapering to 0 at bay 3.52)
```

Unchanged from v0.10.3 (which corrected only the sub-bin stage-storage
inverse; constants from the v0.10.1 refit). Forecasts use raw hourly NWS
QPF; nowcasts use observed MRMS PrecipRate over the catchment. Validated
tank arithmetic is not validated rain-input skill. The nowcast still
holds one bay head through its 45-minute projection; the moving-head
candidate remains HELD (see `archive/v0.10.3.md`).

## Tide-keyed pathway

For each high tide:

```text
SH peak MLLW = astronomical tide + NWS projection or observed surge
water NAVD88 = SH peak MLLW + 0.00 − 2.82
depth at landmark = max(0, water − landmark NAVD88) × 12 inches
```

If live surge is unavailable, production labels the calculation
`astronomical-only-degraded`. Offshore peak winds retain the separately
reported −0.13-ft expected-actual adjustment. The retired cold lockout
remains advisory only.

## Static scenarios

The map slider, rain calculator, and advisory scenarios use the same tank
constants at steady state and the corrected stage-storage inverse:

```text
V_steady = (TANK_K / TANK_KOUT) · net_rate^TANK_GAMMA
```

The saturating tanh estimate remains a labeled conservative comparison.
The v0.9 power law remains only as a legacy comparison; neither drives
the primary hydrograph.

## Calibration evidence and limitations

- Measured peak anchors: six — December 19 and October 30, 2025; July 6,
  July 9, July 18, and August 3, 2026.
- Full fit hydrographs: two — July 6 and July 9, 2026.
- Independent peak checks: October 30 and December 19, 2025.
- Independent measured recession constraint: one — July 18, 2026.
- Out-of-sample hindcast: August 3, 2026.
- Post-cutover measured Events 7–9 remain recipe hindcasts, not additions
  to the frozen calibration set.
- Reproduction: the numeric production replay is unchanged —
  [`history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_3.py`](../../history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_3.py)
  against [`model/data/v0.10.3-reproduction.json`](../data/v0.10.3-reproduction.json)

  exists); the legacy v0.10.1 record replays via
  [`history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_1.py`](../../history/scripts/reproduce_v0_10_1.py).

Known structural gaps remain deliberately untuned: event-dependent lag,
antecedent wetting, bidirectional drainage and hysteresis, a tilted
recession pool, recirculation between grates, long hillside delivery, and
the sidewalk swale micro-basin. Future changes must be evaluated one
mechanism at a time and versioned explicitly, with independent candidate
review and a recorded owner decision before promotion (AGENTS rule 12).
