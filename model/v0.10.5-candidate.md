Hourly-run acquisition: five quick requests under one 60-s monotonic deadline
(15 s per request). The expensive sources come from
`data/outlook_guidance.json` (warm job every 3 h). Alerts never wait on any of
this.

### Precedence

- **Per high tide (cards, table, ledger):** nws_product → nwps (≤ 72 h, shadow)
  → petss midpoint (≤ 102 h, band shown) → persistence decayed e^(−lead/48 h)
  (assumption, shadow-scored) → astronomy labeled "no surge guidance".
- **Continuous hourly line (chart, maps):** CFW rows ANCHOR the line; NWPS
  supplies the hourly shape between anchors (additive correction, held 1 h,
  interpolated between anchors, fading over 6 h beyond the first/last); beyond
  NWPS: astronomy + the per-tide ladder surge interpolated.
- **Rain per hour:** NWS grid inside its reach → NBM 6-h uniform rate → WPC
  24-h uniform rate → unknown (flagged, never zero). Buckets are [start, end).
  Day totals are prorated by overlap with the station-local day.
- **Production (unchanged from v0.10.4):** per tide, product row within 2 h,
  else surge persistence, else astronomical-only-degraded; the 30-h series
  still applies the worst tide's surge as a constant (recorded residual).

### Horizon and scope
- Outlook: seven station-local calendar days; hourly series from −6 h to the
  end of day 7 (exclusive); cards and series share that scope.
- Alerts (ntfy/email): tides ≤ 48 h ahead only (`ALERT_WINDOW_HOURS`); SMS is
  nowcast-only. Display horizons (72-h rollup, 7-day outlook) never widen
  alerting.

### Rain scenarios
- Tank line: the production tank integrated on the tide+surge curve.
- Burst scenario: analog 1.7 × (max 6-h / 0.55) capped 3.0 in/hr when
  burst-capable hours or convective wording exist; potential at the fixed
  2.5 ft NAVD88 low bay (rain-alone view), at each burst-capable hour's own
  tide level, and at the day's high tide (compound view).
- NBM 90th-percentile scenario: the same analog on the p90 6-h amount, shown
  as the rain band's labeled high end with its exceedance chance; it does not
  drive the headline (mirrors the P-ETSS high end).
- Summed 6-h percentiles are not daily percentiles and are named as sums.

### Day-scoped worst pathway (headline rule, AGENTS rule 6)
`day_worst` = max over {tide regime of the day's tides; regime of the day's
highest continuous water; burst-potential regime on burst-capable hours;
categorical rain watch at rank 1}. Landing cards, ribbon, today's headline
and the email subject read it. Widget keeps its owner-set tidal-days /
rain-days lines.

### Shadow scoring and promotion policy
`data/outlook_log.csv` (append-only) logs every source per tide per run.
Scoring: one observation per tide per lead bucket (issuances averaged),
MAE/bias across tides, candidate paired with baseline on identical tides;
READY requires 28 distinct tides and a lower MAE; promotion of any shadow
source into production remains human-gated (rule 12).

### Confidence labels
Retired from every human surface and from `forecast.json`; ledgers keep the
column and write it empty; the measured error by lead replaces them, named
as Sandy Hook tide-peak gauge skill.
