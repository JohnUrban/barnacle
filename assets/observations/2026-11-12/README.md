## What this does and does not show

John looked 80 minutes after the peak and about half an hour after the bay
had dropped back below the SW grate. So the report establishes that nothing
was standing above the grates at 10:50. It does **not** establish whether
shallow water reached the street between 08:12 and 10:18: a few inches over
the grate could have come and drained back before he looked, and with rain
starting, wet pavement no longer tells anything.

It is consistent with the gate holding. It is not the direct test that an
observation near 09:30 would have been. Barnacle's gauge forecast was right
to within 0.01 ft; whether its street call was right is still unobserved.
