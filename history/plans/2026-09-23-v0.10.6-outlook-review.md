- **A. Low-tide surge score (history).** `surge_decay_views.py` gains a
  VIEW for target hours at astronomical low tides (local minima of the
  prediction). Same method as the other views.
- **B. Controlled wet scenarios (sensitivity, NOT skill).** Real hourly
  astronomy (Sandy Hook, 2025-10-01..03), a reading s in {+1.0, +2.0} ft at
  issuance, mean +0.54 ft, a 3-h rain burst of 0.3 / 0.6 / 1.0 in/h placed
  at lead 6 / 12 / 24 h on the nearest astronomical low, rising-mid and high
  tide. Identical rain for v0.10.5 (constant surge) and v0.10.6 (decay).
  Compare the production tank's peak (inches over the SW grate), minutes
  above the curb (+7.7 in) and the lawn step (+13.7 in), and landmark depths.
- **C. Measured events, reconstructed rain (hindcast, NOT as-issued skill).**
  Events with cached MRMS box-mean frames and a measured peak: 2025-10-30,
  2026-07-06, 08-03, 08-07, 08-27 (if a measured peak exists), 09-01, 09-13.
  Rain = MRMS box-mean (identical for all bays). Bay curves: observed (the
  reference), v0.10.5 constant and v0.10.6 decay from the observed surge at
  issuance 6 / 12 / 24 h before rain onset (mean = trailing 365-d mean at
  issuance). Compare bay error at the event and tank-peak error vs the
  measured peak. The known lag/burst biases are shared by all bays and are
  not what is being compared.
- **D. As-issued Sep 13 (partial as-issued inputs).** The 2026-09-13T03:14:36Z
  forecast (commit 3a6c96faf) saved hourly QPF for its tides' rain windows.
  Run the tank on those saved QPF hours with each bay curve; hours the
  windows do not cover are ZERO by assumption and reported; tank starts empty
  at the first covered hour. Compare with the measured +13.7 in.
Success is not a pass/fail on skill: the question is whether v0.10.6 changes
