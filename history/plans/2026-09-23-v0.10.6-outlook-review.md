## Why this belongs in the bump

The seven-day chart uses different surge construction from the repaired
production curve. In the public 2026-09-24T03:24:52Z forecast, its surge
above astronomy spans 1.57-1.95 ft on Sep24, 1.76-2.54 ft on Sep25,
and falls from about 1.07 ft at Sep28 00:00 station time to 0.25 ft at
09:00. These are derived forecast values, not observations. The latter
fall includes interpolation from P-ETSS guidance into assumed decay.
The graph's smooth appearance does not establish a weather-driven retreat.

build_series, _surge_at and _anchor_correction. Rendering was repaired in
0b9c4404a; that fix did not change these numbers.
